Independent studios, shared exhibitions
More than 30 practising artists across textiles, sculpture, photography and painting have been exhibiting together for over a decade. Each member works from their own studio, in their own medium, and the group comes together regularly to critique, problem-solve and build shows collaboratively, rather than under one house style.
